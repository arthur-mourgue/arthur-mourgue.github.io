LePotager

In 48 hours at a LeRobot hackathon, we built a robot arm that sorts fruit by pressing it. Colour can lie about ripeness, touch does not.

The policy is an imitation learning model trained on camera images and a low-cost pressure sensor. We also learned that too many recovery demonstrations taught the policy to miss the fruit.

LaMain

An open-source three-finger hand for the SO-101 arm, designed with Julien Navet. It is designed to stay under 200 grams so the arm can still lift objects, and it carries touch sensors on the fingertips.

The goal is to train robot learning models with touch, from imitation learning to vision-language-action models.

Capra Robotics

Six months on autonomous mobile robots in Denmark. I trained a segmentation model that lets the robot see obstacles its LiDAR misses, on a dataset built with ten times less manual labelling.

I also moved face and plate anonymisation inside the camera, and built the localisation that lets the robot dock to a food cart, 18 times out of 18 in tests.

Safran

At Safran, flight-test engineers can program an on-board computer, but only in C. The project lets them describe what they want in plain English.

A local language model turns the request into blocks they can check and edit, and the blocks become embedded C++.
